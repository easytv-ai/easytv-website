---
title: Team
description: Team space, invites, seats. Generation spends the team pool only when EasyTV-Team is set.
---

Needs [auth](/docs/api-auth).

- Missing / `EasyTV-Team: 0`: personal space, own wallet.
- `EasyTV-Team: <team_id>`: team space, freeze from the owner pool.

A user belongs to one team. Buying a team plan makes you owner; seats come from the order (2–10).
