---
title: Partner console
description: /user/partner/console/* for easytv-partner. Same login as the studio; data is scoped to the caller.
---

No separate partner account. Partners sign in with their EasyTV user. Nicknames are masked; no customer mobile / email / order lines.

Not applied: `success=false, code="not_applied"`. Not approved: `stats` / `customers` return `code="not_approved"`. Rebate balance is CNY converted from user `quota`, **not** studio credits.
